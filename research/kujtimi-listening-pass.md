## Listen pass (understand_media)
- Lead: lyrical dramatic tenor, rich/warm, soft reedy edge, mature (35–50), "decades of
  training", fluent Kosovo Gheg Albanian, folk-art ballad delivery with light operatic
  technique, wide controlled vibrato on held notes. NOT casual pop crooning.
- Female backing: wordless ethereal harmony, mixed far below the lead, choral warmth,
  most prominent on choruses.
- Instrumentation: acoustic 1980s studio arrangement — string section core, brushed
  percussion, upright bass, quiet accordion filigree. No synths. Warm tape saturation.
- Hook confirmed verbatim: "Melodi, kujtimi tash më zgjon" (first chorus).
- Overall: near-perfect match for Radio Kosova in-house light-music output, 1980–85.

## Measurement pass (librosa, same method as Marimanga/Lindja)
- Tempo: listen ~75; beat-track 152 (double-detection); prior-fitted (start 75) 78.3.
  Verdict: slow ballad, ~76 ± 2 BPM.
- Key: G minor, Krumhansl corr 0.968 (strong).
- Bass-region pitch-class histogram: A# 725, G 599, F 482, D# 417, C 388, D 376 —
  i–III–VII-ish motion in G minor, consistent with strings + upright bass ballad.
- Acoustic profile alone cannot name the singer. It does set bounds: mature male,
  Gheg tenor, folk-operatic school.

## Singer-ID check: Baki Jashari — REJECTED
Hypothesis (mine, Aug 17 03:55): description names Selvete Krasniqi + Baki Jashari as
"archive staff", but in Radio Kosova staff and performers were often the same people.

Biographical check (Wikipedia EN, 2026-08-17):
- Baki Jashari, born 1960 in Pristina. Composer, conductor, music educator.
- Studied composition at Ljubljana Academy, graduated 1984. In Ljubljana (academy
  string orchestra) 1990–2000. Director of the Professional Choir of RTP Pristina
  1987–1990.
- Age check FAILS: 21 years old at the 1981 recording; the voice reads 35–50.
- Career check FAILS: composer/conductor, not a pop crooner; in Ljubljana through 1984.
- His RTP connection (choir director, 1987–90) plausibly explains how his name lands
  on archive items — but as a later, conducting role, not as the 1981 crooner.

Verdict: the archive named its own staff (retrieval credit), not its own singer.
The name on the item is a real, prominent musician — but the wrong decade and the
wrong role. Kujtimi stays an unnamed 1981 Radio Kosova ballad; profile on file if a
reference set of 70s/80s Kosovar tenors ever shows up.
