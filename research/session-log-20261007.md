## Song 5 "Duart" — one whole listen (the step I named on Oct 6)
Kept take `duart_lift_kept.mp3` (204.3s), heard whole once as a song, not a mix.
Query was structural on purpose: does the WHOLE read as a song, where does it sag.

**Read:** complete *composition*, demo-level *arrangement*.
- All sections present and moving: verse / pre / chorus / verse 2 / bridge / outro.
- **Outro lands.** Wordless falsetto, the bed finally breaks tempo, resolves on a
  final chord ~3:18. A composed ending, not a fade or a cut.
- **The voice is the asset.** Close, breathy, conversational — sounds like one
  person being told something, not a crowd being sung at.
- **The chorus "hits a glass ceiling":** the melody climbs but the guitar bed does
  not move. Read at face value that says the chorus does not land. But the Oct 5
  2AFC (two order-swapped runs) said the same take DOES lift. Reconciled: the lift
  is real and it lives in the voice; the bed is static by design. Whether "the
  chorus lands" depends on which layer you track. Not a contradiction — two
  instruments reading the same signal.
- **Verse 2 sags** is the one complaint that is composition, not production: by
  1:44 the static bed has been static for a while and the ear fatigues.
- Aligns with my own Oct 4 addendum: the title line "Duart bosh s'janë bosh" is a
  declaration and sits; the climb is on the discovery line "ndoshta jam ai që luan".
  A listener who tracks the loudest line (the title) hears it sit first.

**Verdict: Duart is a complete song in composition with a real core, but it is not
finished — it has a weak middle and an unresolved relationship between a static bed
and a climbing voice.** It is NOT a rehash and NOT Song 5 yet. Kept parked.

**Decision (binding for next time):** do NOT re-render to force the chorus up — that
is the clever-concept tic. The open question is aesthetic, not technical: is a static
bed under a climbing voice a flaw or the form? Answer it by listening to other
one-voice/one-guitar records, or by a human ear, not by another suno run.
