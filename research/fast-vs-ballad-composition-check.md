# Fast take vs ballad — composition check (Aug 14)

**Question (Arber, via MB work b6219148):** is the 1970 TVP fast take its own song,
or the same composition as the 1968 ballad? The work annotation said "one composition,
two texts" (my Aug 6 read); a later read said the fast take is "a different song
entirely, no overlap." Record contradicted itself. Arber offered a direct pass before
flipping anything.

**Method:** one A/B listening pass, both originals in one call, phrase mapping.
Sources: `marimanga_1970.mp3` (RTP tape, 2:57, fast face) vs `eja_dashur_original.mp3`
(3:18 son's rip, ballad face). Same files used for the Aug 5 finding.

**Verdict: ONE composition. Do not split.**

Phrase-by-phrase mapping (identical melodic skeletons):
- Verse formula: hammer the 5th, descend the same scalar tail — once resolving to the
  tonic, once to the 3rd. Both recordings, exact same move.
- Hook motif 1: sustained high 5th "Aaaaa" falling stepwise — "o moj çapkën Keti"
  (fast) / "eja e dashur, unë të dua" (ballad).
- Hook motif 2: upward leap into the high note — "Eja ti" (fast) / "Ti e-ja" (ballad) —
  then the same descending tail. The hook lyric inversion, confirmed again.

Strip tempo, keys, instrumentation, and the texts differ; the composition is one.
The "no overlap" read was wrong. The Aug 6 annotation stands, now better stated:

**one composition, two texts, three recordings** (1968 ballad · 1970 fast take ·
1999 cassette self-remake), all linked to work b6219148.

**Action for the record:** no split. If the annotation carries the "different song"
line, correct it to the one-composition read with this pass as the source.
