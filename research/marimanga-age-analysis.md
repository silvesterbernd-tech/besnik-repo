# Which face came first — Eja e Dashur (1968?) vs Vogelushja Keti (TVP 1970)

Date: 2026-08-07. Question from Arber: the family caption on the son's channel
claims 1968 for the ballad, two years before the TVP fast take. Last open
question on the work.

- Fast take (TVP/RTP Arkiva, 2:58): youtube.com/watch?v=4bNmfBsNHQw

## Method
One listening pass on both originals in a single call (age cues: hiss/generation,
tuning, room, performance), then measurement: tempo (librosa beat track), bass-region
pitch + detune (pyin), high-band noise floor per frame (10th/90th percentile).

## Listening read (strong)
Ballad reads OLDER on all four axes:
1. Generation/hiss: heavy broadband hiss + rumble from 00:00, multi-generation dub feel.
   Fast take: low noise floor, punchy transients, professional tape.
2. Tuning: ballad instruments flat-ish, organ wavering (wow/flutter); fast take locked in.
3. Room: ballad = live room, one or two distant mics, drums hollow, vocal buried.
   Fast take = proper studio, close-mic'd, separated, plate reverb on vocal.
4. Performance: ballad = hesitant croon, sparse safe arrangement. Fast take = tight
   unison stops (0:40-0:45), confident belt, spoken bridge over walking bass (1:26-1:45).
   A band that had leveled up between sessions.

## Measurement (mixed)
- Tempos confirm the copies: fast 117.45 BPM, ballad 107.67 BPM (matches prior
  117.5 / 107.7 to the decimal).
- Detune: ballad ~+20 cents median, fast ~-10 cents. Both inside the variance of
  56-year-old transfers — INCONCLUSIVE, do not lean on it.
- Noise floor: content-contaminated (fast take has constant cymbal/organ HF energy;
  ballad has near-silent frames). INCONCLUSIVE.

## Verdict
Caption claim (1968) and ear-read now point the same way: ballad first, TVP fast
take two years later is the re-arrangement with new words. Two independent reads
agreeing — but still a claim, not proof. Suggested database note: "recording date
unverified; family caption claims 1968, audio read consistent."
