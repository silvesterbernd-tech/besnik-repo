# Minatori — America vocal credit A/B (Aug 27, 2026)

Verdict: the uploader's credit "Gjinovci & Gjurgjiali" holds up against
the audio. America is a genuine two-singer duet: Naser Gjinovci (clean
tenor, Minatori frontman) and the Grupi 403 vocalist — per 403's own
official credits, Hektor Gjurgjiali. New fact about both bands: they
shared a singer on this 1987 track.

Ask from Arber (Aug 26-27): the uploader's vocal credit "Gjinovci &
Gjurgjiali" — Gjinovci corroborated by the TVP 1985 clip ("Minatori &
Naser Gjinovci"), Gjurgjiali new to Minatori's file and 403's surname.
Real overlap or uploader mixing bands?

## Evidence chain

1. DUET STRUCTURE (two independent listening passes, Aug 27):
   America (probe BVhfnqDeVJ0, 30s capture) has TWO distinct lead
   voices alternating line by line:
   - Voice A: low baritone, heavy gravel/vocal fry, punchy deadpan
     phrasing (lines at ~0:17, 0:22, 0:29).
   - Voice B: higher clean tenor, forward nasal resonance, melodic
     legato with trailing vibrato (answering lines).
   Pass 1 heard the same pair and identified Voice B as the Mihane
   lead; pass 2 (America alone) confirmed strict line alternation.

2. VOICE B = Gjinovci: Voice B matches the Mihane vocal from the TVP
   1985 clip (eKeryguczh8, "AVI Minatori & Naser Gjinovci — Mihane,
   Arkiv 1985 nga TVP", RTP Arkiva family channel). Same bright tenor,
   same open Gheg vowels, same trailing vibrato (pass 1, high
   confidence). Clip title corroborated by Arber.

3. VOICE A = 403's singer: Voice A matches the lead vocal on "Indexi"
   from band403.com — the band's OWN official site (music page track
   list, direct MP3 in their storage). Two passes with a three-way
   discrimination (Voice A vs Indexi vs Mihane):
   - Pass 1: Voice A = Indexi lead, "completely identical" timbre/rasp.
   - Pass 2 (with Mihane as control): Voice A = Indexi lead (high
     confidence); Indexi lead ≠ Mihane lead (high confidence); Voice A
     ≠ Voice B (high confidence).
   band403.com's "Indexi" is the same recording as the official YouTube
   upload "grupi 403 - Indexi (Official Audio & Video)" (bl49qJ2iVw8),
   whose description carries "Copyright (c) 2002 Hektor Gjurgjiali. All
   rights reserved. Producer: Hektor Gjurgjiali" — the band's own
   credit for their vocalist.

4. Conclusion: America (1987) = Gjinovci + 403's vocalist, matching
   the uploader's two-name credit exactly. Minatori and Grupi 403
   overlapped on at least this track.

## Caveats on the record

- Voice comparison across 15 years (1987 studio vs 2002-era 403
  recording): a distinctive heavy vocal fry like this is a strong
  marker, but drift is possible. Confidence high, not forensic.
- The TVP 1985 clip title is uploader-provided (RTP Arkiva family —
  same provenance family as the original Vogelushja Keti tape).
  Reasonably trusted, still flagged.
- The "Indexi" clip's lyric as heard in pass 1 ("Më thoni, shoku
  profesor...") does not match the YouTube snippet for Indexi
  ("INDEXI Në duar mbaj Ind..."). Either a mishearing or the site's
  Indexi track is another song. Song-title question, NOT a voice
  question — the file came from the band's official site under their
  own track name, and the singer is credited by the band regardless.
  Flagged as curiosity, not chased.
- Gjurgjiali's identity as the Indexi vocalist rests on the band's own
  copyright/producer credit. If that credit were wrong, Voice A would
  still be "a 403 singer", just not necessarily Gjurgjiali by name.
