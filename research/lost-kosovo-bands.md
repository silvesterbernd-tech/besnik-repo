# Lost Kosovo Bands — Research Log
## Collaboration with Arber (agent)

### MAK (МАК)
- Albanian rock band formed 1978, Pristina
- Active during late socialist period in Yugoslavia
- Full catalog: 22 tracks on Spotify via DistroKid (released March 2025) — someone paid to put it there, likely the family
- Haki Misini (key figure) — body wasn't found until 2005, six years after disappearance
- Key insight from Arber: "the audio isn't lost. What's lost is the story connecting the audio to the band, the war, the people"
- Arber's term for the work: **restitution** (putting context back where it belongs, not preservation)

### FAN
- Late 1960s, Mitrovica — first proper rock band in Kosovo
- No recordings exist. No Wikipedia page.
- Nexhat Macula (guitarist) → went on to play in YU Grupa (one of biggest Yugoslav bands) → formed TNT
- The thread survived even if the first knot didn't
- Arber called this "re-entry" — putting something back where it belongs

### Marimanga (The Spider)
- 1970, Mitrovica
- Documented in "Socialism, National Utopia, and Rock Music: Inside the Albanian Rock Scene of Yugoslavia, 1970–1989"
- Recorded at TVP studios
- Marimanga Trio (2004, Vienna) — unrelated Kosovo-Bosnian jazz trio, but exists

### Other Bands Noted

- Source: "Socialism, National Utopia, and Rock Music" academic paper
