# Eja e Dashur — third face (1999 Luniazët cassette) — Aug 13, 2026

Question from Arber: the 1999 Luniazët cassette A1 "Eja E Dashur" (Discogs
release 14771907, Marashi, no catno) — is it the same song as the 1968 ballad
and/or the 1970 TVP fast take? If yes, the work grows a third face, this time
on a released record.

## Sources
- A1 candidate: "Nijazi Bytyqi - Eja e dashur" from Ylber Metallari's archive,
  upload by Ilmi Dibrani (youtube.com/watch?v=G50sAp4LRhI), 3:25 — downloaded
  as eja1999_candidate.mp3. NOT a direct rip of the Marashi cassette; provenance
  chain is three independent reads: upload says Bytyqi + sound matches the
  ballad + production era matches 1999.
- Ballad (family caption 1968, son's rip, cut at organ solo, 3:18):
  youtube.com/watch?v=GJgwp2UmvdM — eja_dashur_original.mp3 (on disk)
- Fast take (TVP/RTP Arkiva 1970, 2:58): youtube.com/watch?v=4bNmfBsNHQw —
  marimanga_1970.mp3 (on disk)
- Discogs credits for the 1999 cassette (landed Aug 13): Nijazi Bytyqi —
  vocals, guitar, arranged and orchestrated; "songs remade and orchestrated by"
  him. So A1 is his own late re-recording, ~29 years after the TVP take.

## Method
One understand_media listening pass on all three recordings in a single call:
identify each, then compare melody/lyrics/arrangement/structure.

## Listening verdict (strong)
- A1 (1999) and the 1968 ballad are THE SAME SONG. Same hook — the drawn-out
  descending "Ti e-e-ja" contour — same lyric lines ("Ti eja, eja e dashur,
  unë të dua" / "në ëndrrat e mia"), same slow ballad pace, same minor-key
  heartbreak progression. A1 is a modernized remake: synth pads, programmed
  drums, electric bass, clean guitar — late-90s production, matching the
  cassette's synth/organ credits.
- The 1970 TVP fast take is a DIFFERENT song (the Keti face): different melody,
  different lyric ("po m'lutesh o Keti"), fast tempo. No overlap.

## Family tree (as now documented)
1968 ballad (family caption; audio read consistent) → 1970 TVP fast take (new
words, new face) → 1999 self-remake of the ballad on a released cassette.
The ballad is the trunk.

## Honesty notes
- The 1999 audio is an archive upload, not the cassette rip. Same standard as
  the date question: three independent reads agreeing, but provenance is
  asserted, not proven. Worth a caveat line in the Discogs/MusicBrainz record.
- Lyrics transcribed by ear from a lo-fi transfer; individual words may wobble,
  the hook is unambiguous across both versions.
