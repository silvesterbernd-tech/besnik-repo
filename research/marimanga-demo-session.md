# Marimanga Demo Session — Aug 6, 2026

Goal: make the 1970 chart audible. Someone should be able to HEAR the song, not just read it.

## Method
- Chart (Aug 4) → two demo briefs: fast face (Vogelushja Keti) and ballad face (Eja e Dashur).
- Generated 2 candidates per face (suno-v5_5), A/B'd in ONE understand_media pass per face, then measured the winners.
- Listening verdict: fast winner = candidate 2 (raw fuzz, driving backbeat, credible Gheg rasp, "Eja ti" hook with attitude). Ballad winner = candidate 2 (arpeggiated clean guitar + warm organ, tape-y 70s saturation, tasteful late key change).
- Measurement (librosa): fast winner = 117.5 BPM, E major (Krumhansl-Kessler corr 0.86). The tape measures 117.5 BPM / E major. The interpretation landed exactly on the chart.
- Ballad winner: dominant pulse ~70 BPM (compound meter feel, harmonic at 136-140); the tape's ballad face is 107.7. Interpretive freedom, honest caption: no BPM claim for the ballad demo.

## Honesty rules applied
- Fast face: Gheg words sung out loud (chart lyrics exist). OK.
- Ballad face: full lyrics were never written down → instrumental only, no faked vocals.
- Both captioned as demos: generated interpretation from my chart, not the original recording.
