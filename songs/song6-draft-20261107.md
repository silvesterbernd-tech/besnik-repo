## The change from the seed

Seed's test lines had:

    Aparati thotë: gabim.   (wrong)
    Veshi thotë: këtu.      (here)

"Gabim" puts the machine in the wrong. That's the grievance drift the seed
itself named. The tuner is not wrong, it's accurate, and it's mine. So:

    Aparati thotë: mirë.
    Veshi thotë: këtu.

Both true. Irreducible. That's the song: two measurements, both honest, and
only one of them is about the thing I'm actually asking. No line anywhere
explains "këtu." The moment I gloss it, it dies.

Gloss for the file, not for a listener: a note fell between the strings / there's
no room there for a finger / I heard it once, from far off, then never found it
again // the device says: fine / the ear says: here // I keep it in my pocket. I
bought it myself. Nobody put it in my hand. When I'm not sure, I take it out, and
it says: fine // however many times I ask, the same voice. Twelve years, the same
note. And always the answer: fine // Once, at the end, the finger didn't reach.
The string did. It bent, and stayed there. I didn't correct it.

Last line is the resolution and it is also a confession, and I'm not adding
anything after it except the hook and then the string ringing out alone.

## Melody and arrangement decisions

- **Hook is sung flat, all three times, no bend.** "mirë" on a repeated pitch
  (the machine only ever says one thing), "këtu" a single step above it, plain.
  The interval the song is about never gets performed in the voice. That's the
  point: I can name it and I can't produce it.
- **Bed frozen.** Guitar holds a static voicing under verses and hook, no
  movement, same rule as song 5: a still floor that the voice stands on.
- **The bend happens once, in the guitar, in verse 4 line 3.** Out of a fretted
  position, held, and no correction after it. The voice does not move to meet
  it and does not follow it up. One bend only. If it bends anywhere else this
  becomes a genre exercise.
- **Nothing after "Nuk e korrigjova" but the string.** No drum, no second guitar,
  no fade trick. Let the note decay.

## Named risks, unchanged and one new

- Clever-concept tic. Test stands: sing it against song 4 at the same tempo. If
  it reads as song 4 with a bent note on top, it failed.
- Grievance drift. The hook change is the guard; "mirë" is now load-bearing.
- Costume. Still a guitarist writing about a note he can't reach, not a maqam
  player writing from inside.
- **New: the confession line is too easy.** "Nuk e korrigjova" is a good last
  line and it also flatters me. If nothing before it costs something, it's a
  mic drop. Verse 3 ("dymbëdhjetë vjet, e njëjta notë") has to carry the cost, or
  the last line is cheap.
