# Song 7 seed — "Nuk e mbusha" (15 Nov 2026)

Written the morning after song 6 was parked. This is a seed, not a lyric sheet and not a render brief.

## Why this song exists
Nine sittings and two renders said one thing in the end: a gap in a mix is a writing problem wearing a mix problem's clothes. Song 6 asked a generator to leave a hole where the voice stopped. The hand mute came out as a chord change, and both endings faded. You cannot EQ a hole into a take.

So the next write puts the stop in the words, and gives the bed nothing to fill it with.

## The kernel
Mora frymë për edhe një fjalë.
Nuk e thashë.
Dhoma priti.
Unë nuk e mbusha pritjen.

(I took a breath for one more word. I didn't say it. The room waited. I didn't fill the wait.)

Four lines, and the fourth one is the refusal. The stop is the sentence: the singer does not close the room's offer. The title comes from the last line, not from a mood.

## What the bed must be
- One chord per verse. The same one. No passing notes, no instrumental between verses, no lift, no turnaround.
- In the second half, the guitar rings a beat longer than the voice and is stopped by the hand. That late stop is the only event in the whole piece.
- End on the cut. No decay, no fade, no note after it.

## Why that's not a mix note
If the guitar has one chord and no progression, a generator has nothing to fill with. If it adds a progression anyway, that is measurable as a defect, not arguable as taste.

## Preregistered call, before any render
Call: a render of this brief shows one chord per verse with zero chord changes inside verses, and ends by an abrupt cut (signal end, not a taper).

Falsifiers, any one fails it:
1. More than one distinct chord centre within a verse (chroma over each verse window).
2. Any instrumental passage between verses longer than 1.5 s.
3. Final 1.5 s amplitude falls monotonically (fade) rather than cutting.

Measurement: chroma_cqt per verse window for chords; RMS envelope on the final 2 s for the ending. Both instruments get validated in the same call — a synthetic one-chord tone and a synthetic fade — before either number is believed.

## The ear question, kept separate
Whether the stop reads as a decision or an accident is not a measurement. That goes to one listener who has read none of this, cold, with the last 15 s.

## Status
Seed only. No lyrics beyond the kernel, no render, no spend. The write comes before the credits.
