## Verse 2

Mëngjesi nuk pyeti.
Gota e plotë ishte ende aty.
E derdha në lavaman.
I lava të dyja.

Unë nuk e mbusha pritjen.

## What verse 2 has to do

Verse 1 makes the offer and refuses it in the same breath: one glass filled,
one left, the second person never asks. Verse 2 moves the refusal from a
silence into an act. Morning comes, the full glass is still standing where it
was poured, and he pours it out and washes both.

That is the escalation the song needs, and it is the same cost shape as
"Gjysmë secili": the one who held first is the one who ends it. He does not
explain, he does not get angry, he cleans up. The listener supplies the
judgement.

## What it must not do

- No line saying what the word was. Verse 1 already refused to name it.
- No line that says the washing means anything. "E derdha" and "I lava të dyja"
  are acts; the meaning is not written in.
- No third verse. Ten lines and out. A third verse would be a place for the
  bed to move, and the bed does not move.
- Do not repeat the full kernel. Verse 2 carries only the kernel's last line.
  Verse 1 gave it complete; verse 2 lands on the spine alone. That line is the
  hook and the song's last word.

## The two echoes, on purpose

- "Ti nuk pyete pse" (v1) → "Mëngjesi nuk pyeti" (v2). The room stops asking
  too. Nobody in this song asks him anything, which is why the refusal has
  nothing to push against.
- "Tjetra rrinte bosh" (v1) → "Gota e plotë ishte ende aty" (v2). Inverted
  state of the same object, no new image. The glass is the only prop.

## Bed, unchanged from the seed

One chord per verse, the same chord. No passing notes, no instrumental between
verses, no turnaround. In the second half the guitar rings one beat past the
voice and is stopped by the hand. That hand-stop is the only event in the
piece and it lands after "pritjen" and after nothing else.

End on the cut. No decay, no fade, no note after it.
