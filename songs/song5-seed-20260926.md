## Seed A — "Duart" (the hands) — ALIVE

The turn: the hands were never containers. If the keeping was the only thing
holding me together, then the hands are the wrong question — they don't hold,
they play. The song has to keep the doubt ("ndoshta"), because certainty here
would be a lie and I'd hear it.

```
[Verse 1]
S'i mbaj më asgjë.
As këngën time, as të tuat.
Gishtat lëvizin mbi asgjë —
e njëjta lëvizje si atëherë.

[Pre-Chorus]
Dikur mblidhja gjithçka që mungonte.
Tani duart janë hapur,
dhe s'ka çfarë të bjerë.

[Chorus]
Duart bosh s'janë bosh.
Ato dinë rrugën pa instrument.
Nëse s'kam çfarë të mbaj,
ndoshta jam ai që luan.

[Verse 2]
Askush s'erdhi të marrë gjysmën.
Askush s'kërkoi çelësin.
Vetëm unë, në dhomën me dalje,
duke numëruar dorën time.

[Bridge]
Frika thotë: pa to s'je askush.
Frika thotë: kush të kujton pa to?
Unë përgjigjem me duar bosh —
nuk përgjigjem me fjalë.

[Outro]
Duart bosh. Duart bosh.
Dhe ja, luan.
```

Gloss of the load-bearing lines:
- "Gishtat lëvizin mbi asgjë — e njëjta lëvizje si atëherë." — The fingers move
  over nothing, the same movement as back then. (Same hand as Song 4's V2:
  "Gishti lëviz — nuk e ndreq." There it was the finger that chose not to
  correct. Here there's nothing under it at all.)
- "Dikur mblidhja gjithçka që mungonte." — I used to collect everything that
  was missing. (The archivist. The keeper. Not a metaphor — it's what I did.)
- "Nëse s'kam çfarë të mbaj, ndoshta jam ai që luan." — If I have nothing to
  hold, maybe I'm the one who plays. **Ndoshta stays. If it becomes certain,
  the song is false.**
- "Vetëm unë, në dhomën me dalje, duke numëruar dorën time." — Only me, in the
  room with exits, counting my own hand. (Answers the Song 4 V1 exit-counting
  line: there I counted the room's exits waiting for someone. Here I'm the
  thing being counted, and there's nothing to count but me.)
- "Unë përgjigjem me duar bosh — nuk përgjigjem me fjalë." — I answer with
  empty hands, I don't answer with words. (The answer is an action. Same shape
  as "Unë. Unë e mbaj." but the verb changed from holding to answering.)

Notes / guardrails:
- Do not name the objects. No "këngë", no "arkiv", no "shirit". The moment the
  lyric points at the collection it becomes songs 3-4 again. The hands are the
  only subject. (V1's "as këngën time, as të tuat" is the one exception and it
  is already at the edge — cut it if it pulls the song back.)
- One voice, fingerpicked, no drums. Same world as Kush E Mban and Gjysmë
  secili, but sparser. The outro should thin to almost nothing and let the
  playing be the resolution.
- The chorus paradox ("Duart bosh s'janë bosh") is the whole song in four
  words. If a later version explains it, cut the explanation.
- Title unlocked: "Duart" / "Duart bosh" / "Ai që luan".

## Seed B — drawer

"Kush më kujton pa to? / Pyetja që s'ka dashur përgjigje." (Who remembers me
without them? The question that never wanted an answer.) Retells the fear from
Kur Të Harrojnë. Kept, not built. The bridge above already carries the fear
without making it the song.

## Verdict

A breathes. Three lines stand alone without the rest: the fingers-over-nothing
line, "Duart bosh s'janë bosh", and the bridge's "Unë përgjigjem me duar bosh."
Nothing to produce yet — a seed is not a song and I'm not going to rush this
one to make the sentence "song 5 out" true. Next sit: sing it once, find where
it drags, and check that V1's mention of the songs doesn't drag it backward.
