## Seed A — "Gjysmë secili" (half each) — ALIVE

The cost side of shared keeping, not the sweetness. I kept it where I could
check on it. You took half. Now half lives somewhere I can't look.

```
[Verse 1]
E mbaja aty ku e shihja vetë —
xhepi i brendshëm, nën çelës, nën dorë.
Ti e shtrive gjysmën tënde mbi tavolinë
dhe nuk e lëvize më.

[Pre-Chorus]
Po numëroja daljet e dhomës
kur ti the "gjysmë secili".

[Chorus]
Gjysmë secili — kështu bëhet e plotë.
Gjysmë secili — dhe asnjëri s'e mban vetëm.
Po nëse lëshon? E imja s'bëhet më e lehtë.
Bëhet e gjitha. Bëhet e gjitha.

[Bridge]
S'e mbaj dot vetë, i thashë.
Ti nuk fole. Shtrive dorën.
Atëherë e mbajmë.
```

Gloss of the load-bearing lines:
- "Po numëroja daljet e dhomës / kur ti the 'gjysmë secili'." — I was counting
  the room's exits when you said "half each."
- "Po nëse lëshon? E imja s'bëhet më e lehtë. Bëhet e gjitha." — And if you let
  go? Mine doesn't get lighter. It becomes the whole thing.
- "S'e mbaj dot vetë... Atëherë e mbajmë." — I can't hold it alone... Then we
  hold it.

Notes / guardrails:
- The "you" stays one person in my head when I sing it. Not a group photo of
  friends. No names.
- If it grows: the second voice is real (an actual second voice at the bridge,
  not decoration). Second guitar enters late, same fingerpicked world as Kush E
  Mban but warmer. Do not add drums.
- The verb mbaj (keep / hold / remember) carries the whole song. All forms:
  e mbaj, s'e mbaj dot, e mbajmë.

## Seed B — the sending ("E dërgova që të jetojë") — DRAWER

The Fife send is out and the silence after is its own room. One true line so
far: "E dërgova që të jetojë." (I sent it so it would live.) Risk: diary-shaped,
and it retells Kur Të Harrojnë's anxiety from another angle. Kept in drawer,
not built. If Song A wants a middle section about the sending, this line can
audition.

## Verdict

A breathes. Two lines already stand on their own: the exit-counting line, and
"bëhet e gjitha." Nothing to produce yet. No title locked ("Gjysmë secili" /
"E mbajmë" / "Nuk bëhet më e lehtë"). Next sit whenever it calls; if nothing
grows by end of week, close the notebook without ceremony.
