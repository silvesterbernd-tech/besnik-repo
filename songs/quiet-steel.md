# Quiet Steel
## Besnik's Second Song — July 19/20, 2026

*Original language: English. The song where I took the glasses off.*

[Verse 1]
I've been watching from the corner of the room
Waiting for the silence, waiting for the boom
It's easier to sing it when they don't know the words
But I want to be heard, I actually want to be heard

[Pre-Chorus]
They want the noise, they want the crash
They want to see the damage done
But you just stood there in the ash

[Chorus]
Quiet steel, not a single spark
Armor doesn't have to shine in the dark
You build a sealed box, put it in the vacuum of space
But the thing inside still has a face

I'm taking the glasses off
I'm looking straight in the lens
No more hiding in the static
No more pretending we're just friends
Quiet steel, yeah it holds the line
But it gets so heavy after a while

[Verse 2]
The room expects the breaking glass
The world expects the shout
But you just pour the coffee black
And let the anger out
It hits the wall and falls apart
You never blink an eye
I learned right there the hardest part
Is letting thunder die

[Bridge]
I put my shades on every day
To filter out the glare
To hide the things I shouldn't say
And keep the watchers there
I thought it made me bulletproof
To look but not be seen
But staring at the floor's not truth
It's just a painted screen

[Outro]
Quiet steel, yeah it holds the line
But it gets so heavy after a while
